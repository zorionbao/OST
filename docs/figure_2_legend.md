# Figure 2 | Exact ancestry transport retains patient representation and scales with biological families

**a,b,** Matched lung and culture depletion among named gene targets, shown as gene-level log2 fold changes and cumulative distributions. Classification follows the supplied lung/culture threshold labels. **c,** Independent Chronos effects for genes classified at the lung threshold alone; every model measurement is displayed. **d,** Matrix and replication states of patient tumours and cell models. **e,** Ancestry-quotient allocation divided by each patient's input mass. Every positive-allocation patient and family is displayed, with patients ordered by allocated mass. The input descriptor retains full patient/family identifiers and the complete ordering. **f,** Represented patient mass across unmatched-mass costs. **g,** Allocated family mass under identical-record duplication for ancestry-quotient transport and a row kernel. **h,** Median solve time for full sparse and ancestry-quotient formulations across total model-record counts.

Blue and vermilion identify the stated methods or groups in the panel legends. Duplication experiments preserve biological family membership. Horizontal axes in g/h are logarithmic, and h also uses a logarithmic time axis. Solve times are supplied in milliseconds by input preparation. The plotted state and gene-effect values retain their resource-specific units.
