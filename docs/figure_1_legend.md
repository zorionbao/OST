# Figure 1 | Pure-state projection preserves osteogenic identity and corrects mixture response

**a,** Mean compartment programme ranks across measured donor pseudobulks; color and point area encode rank. **b,** Independent-donor matrix absolute error across osteogenic RNA fractions for pathway ranks, fixed CRMP and Pure-state CRMP. **c,** Donor-level RMSE reductions for replication, redox and matrix programmes. Half densities, individual donor points and median/interquartile segments describe the distribution. **d,** Pure matrix predictions from fixed CRMP and Pure-state CRMP; the dotted line denotes identity. **e,** Pure-profile signed errors for Pure-state CRMP. Boxes show median and interquartile range, whiskers extend to 1.5 interquartile ranges, and every donor point is displayed. **f,** Mixture RMSE, pure RMSE and score-SD retention for the three methods. Each RMSE is divided by its largest displayed value; score SD is predicted/reference donor SD. **g,** Donor-mean RMSE reductions and percentile bootstrap intervals supplied by the donor-level analysis. **h,** Mean inner-donor loss across discovery holdouts; points identify the evaluated coefficient and drift penalties.

Programme colors are replication, blue; redox, green; matrix, vermilion. Computational mixtures perturb measured profiles, and donors are the validation units. The external input descriptors identify programme/method order, normalization constants, density coordinates, quartiles, box summaries and parameter-grid coordinates. Densities and summaries are calculated during input preparation.
