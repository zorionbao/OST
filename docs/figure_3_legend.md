# Figure 3 | Paired context transfer connects measured dependencies and environmental response

**a,** Within-screen fitness ranks for eligible test genes; columns follow average-linkage Euclidean clustering of gene profiles. **b,** Principal components of the same rank matrix, with plastic cultures shown as blue circles and dome cultures as vermilion triangles. **c,** Model-balanced format and medium rank shifts. ITGB1, PTK2, GPX4 and CDK6 are annotated. **d,e,** Whole-model held-out RMSE for eight estimators. Each line in d and each row in e represents a model. Vermilion highlights PCHT and green highlights global-anchor fusion. **f,** All PCHT held-out observed/predicted gene–pair measurements; hexagon color indicates their count. **g,** Model-balanced shifts across the source-defined pathway classes. **h,** Independent osteosarcoma Chronos effects and organoid-minus-adherent Chronos differences, with color encoding the paired format rank shift and the same four example genes annotated.

Method labels are M1, no change; M2, target mean; M3, paired mean; M4, unpaired mean; M5, nearest pair; M6, global anchor; M7, paired midpoint; M8, PCHT. All conditions of a held-out model are excluded jointly during training. The full-model RMSE aggregates squared errors across all its eligible measurements before taking their square root. Lower fitness ranks indicate stronger depletion.

The nine pathway classes are integrin, extracellular-matrix adhesion, actin regulation, adherens junction, tight junction, lipid metabolism, Wnt signaling, PI3K–MTOR signaling and cell cycle. The external descriptor preserves their exact order and source strings. Sequential color scales increase from white to blue; signed scales run from blue through white to vermilion. The input metadata retain complete gene/screen order, PCA mean and components, and model/method order.
